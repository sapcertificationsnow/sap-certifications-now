What Is C_BCSBS?

The Foundation of the Whole Family

C_BCSBS, “SAP Certified – Positioning the Autonomous Enterprise”, validates that you can explain SAP's Autonomous Enterprise strategy: the operating model, its architectural principles and its value proposition. It is the cross-industry, cross-function member of the 2026 “Positioning SAP Business Suite” family.

Every other exam in the family (Spend Management, Finance, Supply Chain, Customer Experience and more) opens with the same Autonomous Enterprise topic and then applies it to one line of business. C_BCSBS focuses on that foundation itself.

To be transparent: I have not taken C_BCSBS. I took the related C_BCSPM exam twice in 2026, and its first topic is this shared Autonomous Enterprise foundation. The format notes below come from that experience.

What Gets Assessed

The Autonomous Enterprise in 4 Ideas

These are the concepts the shared Autonomous Enterprise topic asked me to explain in the real C_BCSPM conversation.

1

The three-layer operating model

People set direction and keep judgment and accountability; assistants coordinate and give context; agents execute multi-step work within guardrails.

2

The five autonomous domains

Finance, Spend, Supply Chain, Human Capital Management and Customer Experience, and how an event in one domain triggers actions in the others.

3

Governance embedded in execution

Autonomy does not replace governance: agents act within approved business controls and every action is auditable.

4

From pilots to scale

Why AI stays stuck in isolated pilots without connected data, embedded governance and coordinated agents across end-to-end processes.

The habit that scores: open each answer by naming the framework (for example, the three layers), then explain it with one cross-domain example. In my first attempt I skipped the framework and was marked down; in the second I named it up front and the answers held together.

How to Prepare

How to Prepare for C_BCSBS

1

Start with the official learning journey

The badge requires SAP's recommended learning journey. Use it as your source of truth for terms, numbers and the commercial model.

2

Master the three layers and five domains

Be able to draw the operating model on a napkin and explain it in under two minutes.

3

Prepare one cross-domain story

For example, a supplier disruption detected by an agent that triggers actions across Spend, Supply Chain and Finance, with humans keeping accountability.

4

Practise speaking, not reading

Answer sample questions out loud and record yourself. Framework first, outcome second, example third.

5

Pick your next exam in the family

Once the foundation is solid, the line-of-business exams reuse it. C_BCSPM is the procurement version.

FAQ

C_BCSBS Questions

What is the C_BCSBS certification?

C_BCSBS is “SAP Certified – Positioning the Autonomous Enterprise”. It validates your ability to explain SAP's Autonomous Enterprise strategy, its operating model and its value proposition. It belongs to the 2026 “Positioning SAP Business Suite” certification family.

How is C_BCSBS different from C_BCSPM?

C_BCSBS covers the Autonomous Enterprise foundation itself. C_BCSPM applies the same foundation to procurement and spend management, adding the value case, CPO priorities and commercial considerations. See the C_BCSPM exam guide.

Is the C_BCSBS exam multiple choice?

It is a Scenario-Based Assessment. In the related C_BCSPM exam, that meant a spoken conversation with an AI examiner rather than multiple-choice questions. Check SAP Learning for the current format.

Does the C_BCSBS certification expire?

According to the official badge, it stays valid through annual reassessment.

Which certification should I take first?

If you work across functions, C_BCSBS gives you the foundation. If you work in procurement, C_BCSPM applies it to your area. Both share the Autonomous Enterprise topic.
