Chapter 1 Deep Dive

The Three-Layer Operating Model

The official example that scored well: "If a supplier disruption happens, an agent can detect the risk and identify alternative suppliers, launch sourcing activities, verify compliance, and prepare recommendations. Joule coordinates the workflow while procurement leaders focus on judgment and accountability. The process can also trigger actions across Spend, Supply Chain, and Finance — creating an end-to-end operating model instead of isolated automation."

From Real Evaluator Feedback

The Golden Rule

Taken directly from real evaluator feedback: every strong answer opens by anchoring a named framework before explaining. Answers that describe concepts "in your own words," without naming the model behind them, consistently score lower than answers that open, for example, with: "The Autonomous Enterprise runs on a three-layer model: people direct, assistants coordinate, agents execute."
