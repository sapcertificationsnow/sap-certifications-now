Inside the 30 Executive Scenarios

Every scenario follows the same anatomy: company context, the exact question an AI evaluator (or a real client) would ask, and a complete 5-step model answer. Here's one, in full.

Scenario 1 · External Workforce Governance

"We already have consolidated purchasing processes in our ERP."

Company context: A global manufacturer already runs SAP S/4HANA. Over the past few years the company sharply increased its use of contractors and engineering consultants, each region managing them in isolation. The CFO has flagged a lack of global cost visibility, and recent audits found inconsistent onboarding processes.

Question from the CPO"We already have consolidated purchasing processes in the ERP. Why should we invest in another SAP solution?"
1"What you're describing isn't a gap in materials procurement — it's the absence of corporate governance over your external workforce. As contractor and consultant usage grew, the organization lost control, standardization, and compliance at the global level."
2"Because contractors are hired regionally through disconnected spreadsheets and emails, the company is highly exposed to labor liabilities, no financial control over rates, and security gaps around who has access to facilities and systems."
3"I'd recommend prioritizing SAP Fieldglass, since it establishes one standardized digital process to request, qualify, manage, and offboard external workers across every region and business unit."
4"For the CPO and CFO, this delivers 100% cost visibility, guarantees negotiated rate compliance, ensures regulatory compliance before work even begins, and drastically cuts operational and legal risk."
5"I'd prioritize this first because containing labor risk and contractor spend generates immediate savings and compliance protection that your ERP alone was never designed to govern."

Before You Buy

Is this an official SAP publication?

No — this is an independent study guide created by SAP Certification Now, built from the exam's published blueprint, official comparison data, and real scenario patterns. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SAP.

Do I need hands-on Ariba, Fieldglass, or Concur experience?

It helps, but it isn't required. The guide is built around business reasoning and the 5-Step Framework first — the specific SAP solution names are taught alongside each scenario, not assumed as prior knowledge.

What format is the guide delivered in?

A digital eBook (DOCX/PDF-style layout) you can read on any device, with executive tables, scenario cards, and a printable cheat sheet.

How is this different from the Additional Scenarios guide?

This is the foundational guide — the framework, the stakeholder map, the portfolio, and the first 30 scenarios. Additional Scenarios 31–100 is a pure practice extension using the exact same method, for candidates who want more reps before sitting the exam.

Should I get this or the Autonomous Enterprise & Spend guide?

They cover different exam content. This guide targets the classic C_BCSPM functional scenario format (Ariba/Fieldglass/Concur). Autonomous Enterprise & Spend is built from real transcripts of the newer AI-conversation exam format. Many candidates get both — see the bundle.
